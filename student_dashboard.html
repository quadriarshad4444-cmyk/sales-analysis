Key insights

  1. Study hours shows the strongest link with score. Group averages rise from low to high study hours.
  2. Attendance has a modest link with score, with a gap of about 7 points between Excellent and Low.
  3. Sleep shows a moderate association with score. This is not proof that sleep causes higher scores.
  4. Stress, exercise and school type show little difference in average score.
  5. The High study group (24 students) and PhD parent group (24 students) are small, so their averages are less reliable.
